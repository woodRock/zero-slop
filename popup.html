How it works
  1. Labels, not alarms. Flagged posts get a small coloured label next to the timestamp. Click it to see why and to vote on whether it’s right.
  2. Red means slop factory (bot networks, funnels), blue means high AI use, amber means suspicious or unverified, and green means human.
  3. Report from anywhere. Right-click a post and open the ZeroSlop menu to scan or report it.
  4. Private by design. The only thing shared is anonymous slop reports. No personal info, no tracking.
